How to read it

Find a model

Fields

Type, empty or not, key. Every model also has id, created_at and updated_at unless its table says otherwise.

Relations

« belongs to » holds the foreign key; « has many » / « has one » is the other side; « belongs to many » goes through a link table; « morph » relations have no foreign key (owner type + id).

You seeIt means
PK, FK, UKPrimary key, foreign key (→ the model it points to), unique.
Null: yesThe field can be empty. Otherwise it is required.
jsonb · FR / ARA text stored in both languages: {"fr": "…", "ar": "…"}.
ProjectStatusAn enum field (PHP backed enum in app/Enums). Its stored values follow it; hover a value for its screen label.
||, |o, o{, o|In a diagram: exactly one, zero or one, zero or many, zero or one. « Project ||--o{ ProjectMember » reads: a project has zero or many members; a member belongs to exactly one project.
